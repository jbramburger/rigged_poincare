# The Spectral Skeleton of Chaos: Koopman Wave Packets on Poincaré Sections

This repository contains the MATLAB scripts accompanying the paper:

> J. J. Bramburger, S.-S. Gagnon, R. Morris, and M. J. Colbrook,
> **The Spectral Skeleton of Chaos: Koopman Wave Packets on Poincaré Sections**,
> [arXiv:2609.06565](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06565).

The scripts provide data-driven analyses of nonlinear dynamical systems using Koopman operator techniques, with particular emphasis on **measure-preserving extended dynamic mode decomposition (mpEDMD)** and **rigged Dynamic Mode Decomposition (riggedDMD)**.

The central idea is that finite regularized Koopman wave packets provide complementary descriptions of chaotic dynamics. Their phase acts as a finite-resolution transport coordinate, revealing coherent regions and macroscopic cyclic organization, while their modulus identifies a low-modulus **spectral skeleton** near which this phase description becomes poorly resolved. Together, these quantities uncover coarse symbolic dynamics, organize unstable periodic orbits, and describe macroscopic transport on Poincaré sections.

The examples range from a one-dimensional chaotic map to Poincaré maps generated by low- and high-dimensional continuous-time systems. Although the governing equations are known, the Koopman analyses are performed directly from sampled trajectories or Poincaré-section data and do not require closed-form expressions for the corresponding return maps.

## Overview

The repository features five primary scripts.

* **Logistic map (`logistic.m`)**

  * Applies EDMD, mpEDMD, and riggedDMD to a canonical one-dimensional chaotic map.
  * Compares delay-coordinate dictionaries with $d=11$ and $d=51$.
  * Computes spectral densities using separate riggedDMD runs with $\varepsilon=0.01$ and $\varepsilon=0.75$.
  * Selects the finite mpEDMD vector whose eigenvalue is closest to $\lambda=-1$.
  * Uses the wave packet centred at $\theta=\pi$ to reveal an approximate two-region alternation.
  * Computes kneading sequences and empirical transition frequencies.

* **Rössler Poincaré section (`rossler.m`)**

  * Integrates the Rössler system and locates crossings of the section

    $$x=0,\qquad \dot{x}>0$$

    using the ODE solver's event-location procedure.
  * Creates or loads the event-located section cache `rossler_section_cache.mat`.
  * Computes the Poincaré section, scalar return map, and empirical invariant-coordinate histogram.
  * Identifies an approximate fixed point and its nontrivial preimage.
  * Applies riggedDMD to reveal persistent near-threefold spectral organization.
  * Uses the wave-packet modulus to motivate a coarse three-region symbolic model.
  * Compares the resulting symbolic itineraries with periodic orbits observed in the return data.

* **Kuramoto–Sivashinsky Poincaré section (`kuramoto_sivashinsky.m`)**

  * Studies a 32-mode Galerkin truncation of the Kuramoto–Sivashinsky equation.
  * Uses event-located crossings of

    $$a_2=0,\qquad \dot{a}_2>0.$$
    
  * Projects the high-dimensional section data onto its leading uncentred principal-component directions.
  * Identifies a symmetry-induced flip between two components of the first-return dynamics.
  * Removes this immediate alternation by retaining every second crossing and studying the second-return map $F=P^2$ on a single component.
  * Applies riggedDMD to uncover a macroscopic eight-region organization near $\theta=\pi/4$.
  * Connects the resulting transition graph with symbolic cycles and unstable periodic orbits.

* **Forced Duffing oscillator: Regime I (`duffing_regime_I.m`)**

  * Studies the parameter regime

    $$
    \beta=1,\qquad
    \delta=0.3,\qquad
    \omega=1.2,\qquad
    \gamma=0.5.
    $$
  * Generates event-located stroboscopic intersections and stores them in `duffing_section_I_cache.mat`.
  * Identifies a dominant spectral component near $2\pi/3$.
  * Constructs a three-region cyclic partition of the Poincaré section from the wave-packet phase.
  * Quantifies the predominantly cyclic transport between the three regions.
  * Compares the wave-packet phase and modulus with unstable periodic orbits of map periods one, two, and three.

* **Forced Duffing oscillator: Regime II (`duffing_regime_II.m`)**

  * Studies the parameter regime

    $$
    \beta=0.25,\qquad
    \delta=0.1,\qquad
    \omega=2,\qquad
    \gamma=2.5.
    $$
  * Generates event-located stroboscopic intersections and stores them in `duffing_section_II_cache.mat`.
  * Identifies related spectral maxima near $2\pi/7$, $4\pi/7$, and $6\pi/7$, with the $6\pi/7$ component dominant.
  * Constructs a seven-region transport model with the progression

    $$
    R_j\longmapsto R_{j+3}\pmod 7.
    $$
  * Tests the robustness of this transport structure across smoothing parameters.
  * Quantifies the concentration of transport errors near the low-modulus spectral skeleton.

The repository also contains one supplementary example.

* **Forced Van der Pol oscillator (`vanderpol.m`)**

  * Studies a periodically forced Van der Pol oscillator using a stroboscopic Poincaré map.
  * Computes the riggedDMD spectral density and a wave packet near $0.81\pi$.
  * Examines phase-transport branches and their organization on the Poincaré section.
  * Studies low-modulus defect regions, their return statistics, and their transition dynamics.
  * Is included as a record of additional numerical tests but does not appear in the paper.

## Repository structure

* **`logistic.m`** — Logistic-map analysis.

* **`rossler.m`** — Rössler Poincaré-section analysis and event-located data generation.

* **`kuramoto_sivashinsky.m`** — Kuramoto–Sivashinsky Poincaré-section and riggedDMD analysis.

* **`duffing_regime_I.m`** — Forced Duffing oscillator, Regime I.

* **`duffing_regime_II.m`** — Forced Duffing oscillator, Regime II.

* **`vanderpol.m`** — Supplementary forced Van der Pol analysis.

* **`rossler_section_cache.mat`** — Event-located Rössler Poincaré-section data.

* **`ks_section_cache.mat`** — Event-located Kuramoto–Sivashinsky Poincaré-section data.

* **`vdp_psec.mat`** — Cached stroboscopic data for the supplementary Van der Pol example.

* **`metadata/`** — Numerical provenance for the long continuous-time integrations.

  * `rossler_section_metadata.txt`
  * `ks_section_metadata.txt`

* **`ks_upos/`** — Unstable periodic-orbit data for the Kuramoto–Sivashinsky example.

  * `per1sym.mat`
  * `per1asym.mat`
  * `per2asym.mat`
  * `per3asym.mat`
  * `per4.mat`

* **`ks_utils/`** — Auxiliary scripts and data for the Kuramoto–Sivashinsky analysis.

  * `generate_ks_section_data.m` — Generate the event-located Poincaré-section cache.
  * `ks_adjacency_cycles.m` — Compute symbolic cycles predicted by the adjacency matrix.
  * `ks_find_per_3.m` — Identify period-three orbits and their preimages using phase jumps and wave-packet minima.
  * `ks_find_cycles.m` — Search the section data for candidate cycles using return errors.
  * `ks_plot_upos.m` — Plot continuous-time unstable periodic orbits and their projections onto the Poincaré section.
  * `ks_psec_0298_x2=0.mat` — Legacy Poincaré-section data retained for auxiliary comparisons.

* **`duffing_utils/`** — Auxiliary scripts and data for the forced Duffing oscillator.

  * `duffing_upo_search.m` — Locate low-period unstable periodic orbits in Regime I using close returns and shooting.
  * `duffing_upo_plot.m` — Overlay periodic-orbit intersections on the Regime I phase partition and log-modulus field.
  * `duffing_psec_I.mat` — Legacy Regime I section data used by auxiliary calculations.
  * `duffing_psec_II.mat` — Legacy Regime II section data retained for comparison.

* **`duffing_results/`** — Duffing unstable-periodic-orbit data generated by the auxiliary UPO scripts.

* **`vanderpol_results/`** — Figures and supplementary output generated by `vanderpol.m`.

## Dependencies

This repository relies on the **riggedDMD** implementation developed by Colbrook et al.:

https://github.com/MColbrook/Rigged-Dynamic-Mode-Decomposition

To use the scripts in this repository:

1. Clone or download the riggedDMD repository.

2. Copy its `main_routines` folder into the root of this repository, or add that folder to the MATLAB path:

   ```matlab
   addpath('path_to_riggedDMD/main_routines')
   ```

3. Open MATLAB in the root directory of this repository.

The **Optimization Toolbox** is optional for `duffing_upo_search.m`. The script uses `fsolve` when it is available and otherwise falls back to `fminsearch`.

The Kuramoto–Sivashinsky script computes its principal coordinates directly using an uncentred singular value decomposition and does not require the `pca` function from the Statistics and Machine Learning Toolbox.

## Running the examples

The five primary scripts can be run independently from the repository root:

```matlab
logistic
rossler
kuramoto_sivashinsky
duffing_regime_I
duffing_regime_II
```

The supplementary forced Van der Pol test can be run separately:

```matlab
vanderpol
```

### Logistic map

Run

```matlab
logistic
```

to generate the logistic-map eigenvalue, spectral-density, and wave-packet figures.

The script performs separate computations for the requested delay dimensions and smoothing parameters. In particular, it generates:

* `logistic_eigenvalues_1.pdf`
* `logistic_eigenvalues_2.pdf`
* `logistic_measure_1.pdf`
* `logistic_measure_2.pdf`

together with the finite mpEDMD eigenfunction and riggedDMD wave-packet panels.

### Rössler system

Run

```matlab
rossler
```

If `rossler_section_cache.mat` is present and passes the consistency checks, the script loads the cached section data. Otherwise, it integrates the Rössler system and generates a new cache using event-located crossings of

$$
x=0,\qquad \dot{x}>0.
$$

The script generates the Poincaré-section, return-map, histogram, spectral-density, and wave-packet figures. It also writes numerical provenance and diagnostics to

```text
metadata/rossler_section_metadata.txt
```

### Kuramoto–Sivashinsky equation

The event-located Poincaré data can be regenerated by running

```matlab
run('ks_utils/generate_ks_section_data.m')
```

This is a long continuous-time integration and can take considerable time. To avoid storing an unnecessarily large trajectory, the generator advances the integration in blocks and retains only the event-located crossings required for the section data.

The resulting cache is saved as

```text
ks_section_cache.mat
```

and its numerical provenance is written to

```text
metadata/ks_section_metadata.txt
```

Once the cache has been generated, run

```matlab
kuramoto_sivashinsky
```

to reproduce the attractor projection, singular-value spectrum, reduced Poincaré section, spectral density, unwrapped phase coordinate, and wave-packet modulus.

The scripts in `ks_utils/` can then be used to enumerate symbolic cycles, locate candidate periodic points, and plot the supplied unstable periodic orbits.

### Forced Duffing oscillator: Regime I

Run

```matlab
duffing_regime_I
```

The script generates or loads the event-located cache

```text
duffing_section_I_cache.mat
```

and writes the corresponding provenance and diagnostics to

```text
duffing_section_I_metadata.txt
```

It computes the Regime I spectral density, finite wave packet, three-region phase partition, and empirical transition probabilities.

To reproduce the unstable-periodic-orbit overlays, first run the Regime I analysis and then run

```matlab
run('duffing_utils/duffing_upo_plot.m')
```

This produces the six panels used in the paper:

```text
duffing_phase_1.pdf
duffing_phase_2.pdf
duffing_phase_3.pdf
duffing_mod_1.pdf
duffing_mod_2.pdf
duffing_mod_3.pdf
```

If the unstable-periodic-orbit archive is absent, or if the shooting calculation is to be repeated, run

```matlab
duffing_regime_I
run('duffing_utils/duffing_upo_search.m')
run('duffing_utils/duffing_upo_plot.m')
```

The shooting search is substantially more expensive than loading and plotting the existing orbit data.

### Forced Duffing oscillator: Regime II

Run

```matlab
duffing_regime_II
```

The script generates or loads the event-located cache

```text
duffing_section_II_cache.mat
```

and writes the corresponding provenance and diagnostics to

```text
duffing_section_II_metadata.txt
```

The script produces three figures:

* a spectral-density plot showing the three maxima near $2\pi/7$, $4\pi/7$, and $6\pi/7$;
* the Poincaré section partitioned into seven regions by the wave-packet phase;
* the same seven regions mapped forward by one iterate of the Poincaré map, illustrating the approximate $6\pi/7$ phase advance.

The corresponding PDF files are

```text
duffing_II_spectral_density.pdf
duffing_II_phase_partition.pdf
duffing_II_phase_transport.pdf
```

### Supplementary forced Van der Pol oscillator

Run

```matlab
vanderpol
```

If `vdp_psec.mat` is absent, the script generates the stroboscopic data and saves the cache automatically. The supplementary figures and numerical output are written to

```text
vanderpol_results/
```

## Cached data and reproducibility

The continuous-time examples use cached Poincaré-section data because the underlying integrations can be expensive. The principal caches are

```text
rossler_section_cache.mat
ks_section_cache.mat
duffing_section_I_cache.mat
duffing_section_II_cache.mat
vdp_psec.mat
```

The Rössler, Kuramoto–Sivashinsky, and Duffing data are generated using event-located intersections. Their generation scripts record numerical metadata including the integration interval, discarded transient, solver tolerances, crossing or stroboscopic-event rule, number of retained intersections, retained time interval, and event-location residuals.

When a valid cache is present, the corresponding analysis script loads it instead of repeating the integration. To regenerate a particular dataset, delete or rename only the corresponding cache and rerun its data-generation script.

For the riggedDMD calculations, uniform quadrature weights are normalized over the actual number of Hankel snapshot pairs. Spectral densities and finite wave packets that use different smoothing parameters are computed in separate riggedDMD runs.

The reported computations were tested over delay dimensions ranging from approximately $d=20$ to $d=100$. For each delay dimension, the smoothing parameter $\varepsilon$ was varied to reveal the persistent macroscopic structures in the spectral density, phase, and modulus. The particular values of $d$ and $\varepsilon$ used in the displayed figures were chosen to represent these structures clearly; other tested values produce qualitatively similar conclusions.

## Generated output

Most manuscript figures are exported as PDF files directly to the repository root. The principal section caches are also stored in the root directory.

The main exceptions are:

* Rössler and Kuramoto–Sivashinsky metadata, which are stored in `metadata/`;
* Duffing unstable-periodic-orbit data, which are stored in `duffing_results/`;
* supplementary Van der Pol figures and output, which are stored in `vanderpol_results/`.

The scripts use the figure filenames referenced by the manuscript.
